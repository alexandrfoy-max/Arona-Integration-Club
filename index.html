OUR EXPERIENCES

☕ DIY Coffee Experience

10€ / person

A hands-on coffee experience using siphon, filter brewing and other methods. Learn, taste and explore how can you affect your coffee, enjoying calm volcanic desert atmosphere.

  • siphon brewing
  • filter coffee methods
  • tasting & comparison
  • volcanic atmosphere

👉 Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday (and Wednesday morning on request)

🏺 Pottery Workshop

15€ adult | 10€ child

Hands-on pottery class for adults and kids. Create your own piece with volcanic clay. Group up to 10 people — cozy and personal.

  • guided by professional ceramist
  • all materials included
  • take your creation home
  • family friendly

👉 Every Tuesday — adults & children welcome

🗣 Spanish Language Café

Free / Donation

A relaxed language exchange for travelers, expats and locals. Practice Spanish in a natural, friendly environment and meet new people.

  • informal conversations
  • multicultural environment
  • community building
  • no pressure, no rules

👉 Every Tuesday (different time slot than Pottery)

🎨🌿 Eco-Art Workshop

29€ / person

Create art using natural elements from the volcanic landscape. A mindful, eco-conscious workshop connecting creativity with nature.

  • natural pigments & volcanic materials
  • guided by resident artist
  • sustainable art techniques
  • take your unique piece home
Residing artist: Kristina Korbelainen, Finland

👉 Wednesday weekly — deep creative immersion

🌄 Sunset Ritual Experience

19€ / person

A unique sunset gathering on a volcanic landscape. Includes:

  • tea ceremony on the crater of a volcano
  • panoramic views over Los Cristianos, Las Américas, Adeje and La Gomera
  • slow social sunset experience
  • photography & shared moment

👉 Thursday — not a tour, a ritual experience

🎨🍷 Art & Taste

39€ / person | 70€ / 2 persons

The signature weekly experience of Tenerife Integration Club. A curated cultural evening combining:

  • art creation
  • local food & drinks
  • music & atmosphere
  • community gathering

👉 Sunday — flagship experience of the week
