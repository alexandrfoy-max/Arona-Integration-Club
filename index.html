☕ DIY Coffee Experience

10€ / person

Description: A hands‑on coffee journey using siphon, filter brewing and other methods. You will learn how different brewing techniques affect the taste of your coffee. Enjoy the calm volcanic desert atmosphere while tasting and comparing various coffee styles.

  • Siphon brewing demonstration
  • Filter coffee methods (V60, Chemex)
  • Tasting & flavor comparison
  • Volcanic landscape views

Schedule: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday (10:00–12:00). Also available Wednesday morning on request.

Group size: Maximum 10 people.

🏺 Pottery Workshop

15€ adult | 10€ child

Description: Create your own pottery piece using volcanic clay. Guided by a professional ceramist. Perfect for adults and children (family friendly). All materials included, take your creation home after firing.

  • Introduction to hand‑building techniques
  • Working with local volcanic clay
  • Decorate with natural pigments
  • Small group (max 10 participants)

Schedule: Every Tuesday morning 10:00–12:00.

Group size: Maximum 10 people (adults + children combined).

Free / Donation

Description: A relaxed language exchange for travelers, expats and locals. Practice Spanish in a natural, friendly environment, meet new people, and share cultural experiences. No pressure, no rules — just conversation and good vibes.

  • Informal conversations at all levels
  • Multicultural environment
  • Community building
  • Free (donations welcome)

  • 360° panoramic views
  • Sunset photography
  • Community ritual
